Understanding Agentic AI

Agentic AI represents a massive shift from AI that simply "chats" to AI that "acts" to achieve a specific business goal.

"Think of traditional AI (like standard ChatGPT) as a helpful librarian who points you to the right books.

Agentic AI is your expert personal assistant—they don't just find the book, they read it, highlight the key financial metrics, draft a summary, and email it to the investment committee."

The Anatomy of an AI Agent

1. Perception (Sensory System)
Gathers data from the environment—reads emails, scans ERP updates, or monitors market feeds to understand the current situation.
2. Planning (Cognition/Brain)
The strategic command center. Breaks down complex financial goals into smaller, logical tasks and creates an execution plan.
3. Tool Use
The ability to connect to external systems. It can use Excel, log into SAP/Oracle, query a database, or send emails.
4. Action
Executes the plan. Submits the form, routes the approval, or generates the final reporting dashboard.
5. Memory (Context)
Stores short-term memory (the current transaction) and long-term memory (historical vendor terms, company policies).
6. Reflection
Analyzes outcomes. If an action fails or is delayed, it logs the issue and adapts future behavior to be more efficient.

Anatomy in Action: Finance Use Case

Goal: "Process an incoming vendor invoice and schedule it for payment."

1. Perception
Scans the Accounts Payable inbox, detects a new PDF invoice, and uses OCR to extract Vendor ID, Amount, and Due Date.
2. Planning
Creates a checklist: 1. Match with PO in ERP. 2. Verify budget availability. 3. Route to Dept Head. 4. Schedule payout.
3. Tool Use
Accesses the ERP system (to check POs) and the company's secure email server (to route approvals).
4. Action
Sends the approval email, waits for the manager's "Approved" reply, and stages the payment in the banking portal.
5. Memory
Remembers that this specific vendor offers a 2% early-payment discount if cleared within 10 days.
6. Reflection
Logs that the Department Head took 4 days to approve, flagging the timeline for future cash-flow forecasting.
