Use Case 01 · Banking Operations
Loan Origination & Credit Underwriting
An end-to-end banking process evaluating, approving, and disbursing loans — spanning application intake, credit scoring, document verification, risk assessment, compliance checks, loan decisioning, customer communication, and full workflow orchestration. Speed, accuracy, and regulatory adherence are mission-critical.
Process Tasks
T1
Application intake & data capture
iCollect borrower's personal, financial and employment data via digital forms, with AI pre-filling from uploaded documents to minimise manual effort.
T2
Credit score retrieval & bureau checks
iPull CIBIL/Equifax/Experian scores and full bureau reports in real-time to assess historical creditworthiness and existing debt obligations.
T3
Document verification (KYC / income)
iValidate identity proofs, income slips, tax returns and bank statements using OCR and NLP to confirm authenticity and consistency with the application.
T4
Risk scoring & model evaluation
iApply proprietary risk models that combine bureau data, income ratios, employment stability, and collateral valuation to compute probability of default (PD).
T5
Regulatory compliance assessment
iCheck the loan application against RBI guidelines, FEMA regulations, sectoral exposure limits and internal credit policy to flag any regulatory breaches before approval.
T6
Loan decision & terms generation
iProduce a structured approval or rejection decision with the sanctioned amount, interest rate, tenure, EMI schedule and conditions — personalised to the applicant's profile.
T7
Customer communication & Q&A
iRespond to applicant queries about status, required documents and terms via conversational interfaces, reducing branch visits and call-centre load.
T8
End-to-end workflow orchestration
iCoordinate all upstream and downstream systems — CRM, LOS, bureau APIs, compliance engines and communication channels — in a single automated pipeline without human handoffs.

Traditional AI
ML · Rules
Statistical models, decision trees & rule-based engines. Strong at structured data processing but limited on unstructured and generative tasks.
⚙️ Predictive scoring · Classification · Rule matching
✓
iParses structured form inputs, validates field formats and routes data to downstream systems via rule-based pipelines — a well-established ML use case.
✓
iRule-based API calls to bureau endpoints are deterministic and highly reliable — a core strength of traditional integration frameworks.
✗
iLimitation: Document understanding requires reading unstructured PDFs and images. Traditional AI lacks OCR-to-semantic-reasoning capability needed for nuanced KYC checks.
✓
iLogistic regression, gradient boosting, and scorecard models are the industry standard for PD estimation using tabular financial data.
✓
iCompliance rules are codified as hard business rules — deterministic and auditable. Traditional AI excels at rule-matching against policy frameworks.
✗
iLimitation: Generating natural language loan letters and personalised term sheets requires language generation capability absent in traditional ML models.
✗
iLimitation: Conversational NLU is not possible without a language model. Traditional AI can send templated alerts but cannot handle open-ended dialogue.
✗
iLimitation: Siloed ML models cannot orchestrate multi-system workflows. Manual handoffs between teams are required to bridge system boundaries.

Generative AI
LLM · GPT
Large language models that understand context, process unstructured documents and generate fluent text. Limited on real-time data and autonomous action.
🧠 Natural language · Summarisation · Generation
✓
iLLMs can extract fields from free-text emails, PDFs and voice transcripts, dramatically reducing manual data entry at intake.
✗
iLimitation: No live API integration. Gen AI has no access to real-time bureau endpoints; results would be fabricated from training data, which is unacceptable for credit decisions.
✓
iMultimodal LLMs can read, interpret, and cross-validate scanned ID documents, salary slips and bank statements against stated values with high accuracy.
−
iConditional: Can interpret risk scores produced by traditional models and explain them in natural language. Cannot independently compute statistically valid PD without structured model integration.
✓
iCan map application attributes against regulatory text and flag potential violations with cited clauses — very effective when policy documents are provided in context.
✓
iGenerates personalised sanction letters, offer sheets and rejection notices in professional language with contextualised explanations — a top Gen AI strength.
✓
iConversational chatbots powered by LLMs handle complex loan queries, status updates and document requests with high customer satisfaction.
✗
iLimitation: No agency or tool-use. Gen AI can draft instructions and summaries but cannot autonomously trigger API calls, update systems or coordinate multi-step workflows.

Use Case 02 · Risk & Compliance
Fraud Detection & AML Compliance
A mission-critical process identifying, investigating, and reporting suspicious financial activity — combining real-time transaction monitoring, anomaly detection, entity risk profiling, alert triage, SAR/CTR regulatory filings, cross-system data correlation, customer dispute management, and continuous adaptive model retraining.
Process Tasks
T1
Real-time transaction monitoring
iContinuously scan every transaction as it occurs — evaluating velocity, geography, amount patterns and counterparty risk to instantly flag suspicious activity before settlement.
T2
Anomaly & pattern detection
iIdentify deviations from a customer's established behaviour baseline using statistical models and graph analytics to surface hidden fraud rings and mule networks.
T3
Customer & entity risk profiling
iBuild comprehensive risk profiles from KYC data, transaction history, adverse news, PEP/sanctions screening and network linkages — updated continuously as new data arrives.
T4
Alert triage & case investigation
iPrioritise the volume of fraud alerts by risk severity, gather supporting evidence across systems, and produce a structured investigation dossier for analyst review or autonomous resolution.
T5
SAR / regulatory report generation
iProduce Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) and Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs) in the precise regulatory format required by FIU-IND / FinCEN — narrative, evidence, and filing metadata.
T6
Cross-system data correlation
iJoin signals from core banking, card systems, trade finance, CRM, external threat intelligence feeds and sanctions databases to identify fraud patterns invisible in any single system.
T7
Customer dispute & communication
iHandle customer queries about blocked transactions, chargeback claims and fraud notifications with consistent, compliant communication — reducing manual effort in dispute resolution teams.
T8
Model retraining & threat adaptation
iContinuously update fraud detection models with new labelled cases, emerging typologies, and feedback from investigation outcomes — reducing model decay without manual ML engineer intervention.

Traditional AI
ML · Rules
Rules engines and ML classifiers excel at high-speed transaction scoring but struggle with novel patterns, multi-system correlation, and investigative tasks.
⚙️ Scoring · Rule matching · Classification
✓
iReal-time scoring engines processing thousands of transactions per second with sub-millisecond latency is the core strength of traditional ML fraud models.
✓
iStatistical anomaly models (Isolation Forest, autoencoders) are effective at identifying known deviation patterns from established baselines in structured data.
✓
iRule-based risk scores computed from KYC attributes and transaction history are reliable for structured profiling within a single data domain.
✗
iLimitation: Alert triage requires reading unstructured case notes, synthesising evidence and applying judgment — tasks requiring language understanding absent in traditional ML.
✗
iLimitation: SAR narratives must be written in coherent legal language explaining the suspicious activity. Traditional AI cannot generate natural language regulatory reports.
✗
iLimitation: Siloed models cannot join signals across disparate systems without a dedicated ETL/integration pipeline — requires significant engineering not inherent to the AI itself.
✗
iLimitation: No natural language capability. Customer communication is limited to templated rule-triggered alerts without conversational understanding.
−
iConditional: Models can be retrained on new labelled data but requires manual feature engineering, pipeline rebuilding and MLOps processes — not autonomous adaptation.

Use Case 03 · Client Advisory
Wealth Management & Client Advisory
A relationship-intensive financial service delivering personalised investment guidance — combining client risk profiling, portfolio analysis, real-time market research, recommendation generation, MiFID II suitability validation, bespoke reporting, proactive client outreach, and automated portfolio rebalancing with trade execution oversight.
Process Tasks
T1
Client risk profiling & KYC
iAssess client's investment objectives, time horizon, risk tolerance, liquidity needs and tax situation through structured questionnaires enriched with behavioural data and prior engagement history.
T2
Portfolio performance analysis
iEvaluate current portfolio holdings against benchmarks, compute alpha, Sharpe ratio, drawdowns and factor exposures — providing the analytical foundation for all advisory actions.
T3
Market research & intelligence
iSynthesise macro-economic indicators, equity research, fixed income analysis, geopolitical risk and sector news into actionable intelligence tailored to the client's current portfolio.
T4
Personalised investment recommendations
iGenerate bespoke investment recommendations — specific securities, funds or rebalancing actions — aligned with the client's risk profile, goals and current market conditions.
T5
Regulatory suitability checks (MiFID II)
iValidate every recommendation against MiFID II suitability requirements — ensuring the product matches the client's knowledge, experience, financial situation and investment objectives.
T6
Client report & statement generation
iProduce personalised portfolio statements, performance reviews and advisory reports in plain language — replacing generic templated documents with bespoke, insight-rich communications.
T7
Proactive client outreach & Q&A
iInitiate client contact on market-moving events relevant to their portfolio, answer complex investment queries, and schedule advisor calls — acting as an always-on digital relationship manager.
T8
Rebalancing & trade execution
iExecute approved portfolio rebalancing instructions through OMS/trading systems — placing orders, managing execution risk, confirming fills and updating portfolio records with full audit trails.

Traditional AI
ML · Rules
Quantitative models excel at portfolio analytics and risk profiling, but communication, research synthesis, and autonomous execution are beyond reach.
⚙️ Portfolio analytics · Risk scoring · Optimisation
✓
iClustering and classification models effectively segment clients into risk bands using structured questionnaire and demographic data — a well-established quantitative use case.
✓
iQuantitative analytics engines compute all standard performance metrics (alpha, beta, Sharpe, VaR) with precision — the foundational strength of traditional quant models.
✗
iLimitation: Synthesising unstructured research reports, news feeds and analyst commentary requires NLP and language understanding capabilities absent in traditional ML.
−
iConditional: Mean-variance optimisation and factor models generate quantitative recommendations, but they lack personalised language generation and cannot explain rationale in natural language.
✓
iRules engines effectively codify MiFID II suitability criteria as deterministic checks — flagging mismatches between product attributes and client classification with high reliability.
✗
iLimitation: Generating personalised, insight-rich narrative reports requires language generation. Traditional AI can produce data tables but not human-readable advisory content.
✗
iLimitation: No NLU capability. Proactive communication is limited to templated alerts triggered by rule conditions — no conversational understanding for complex advisory Q&A.
−
iConditional: Optimisation algorithms generate rebalancing weights, but trade execution requires system integration and autonomous action capability not inherent to traditional ML models.
